# PRICE-RIDE development protocol

Frozen on 2026-11-23 in configuration `experiment_stage2.yaml`, design `stage2_v1`. Freeze precedes the paired-move diagnostic and comparative methods. No hyperparameter grid is planned. The machine and Python/pip environment are in [starting_evidence.json](stage2_v1/starting_evidence.json). Use Python's seeded random generator, deterministic sort tie-breakers and one BLAS/OpenMP thread. Wall-time cutoffs can alter the last completed batch. Record actual runtime, overrun, iterations, candidate and replay counts.

The unchanged 20/40/60/80 files under both tariffs are the development list. A case needs at least one verified complete start within its bounded feasibility search to enter optimization. A certified infeasible case is listed with its reason. An unresolved case retains every failed start and its planned analysis cells. The 100–200 originals are held out from all routing search and tuning. Only input-derived audit bounds may be calculated for those files. No overlays or alternate constraints are used. The predeclared validation seeds exercise B3/P on development files to check search-seed stability. They provide no held-out instance evidence.

| Method | Search and acceptance |
|---|---|
| B0 | Original Phase 1 constructor; independent replay. |
| B1 | Shared pair moves and global charging repair. Rank and accept by travel plus ride-excess cost, with constant reference energy only as an intermediate ranking feature for B3. B1 selects charging plans by service cost, kWh and earliest timing. |
| B2 | Same routing logic as B1 for the first 75% of total available wall time, followed by full-price fixed-order charging. Total maximum budgets match B1. The fixed-order decoder is deterministic and may finish early. |
| B3 | Shared pair moves, full-price fixed-order decoder, and full monetary acceptance. Rank move previews with a common flat reference rate. |
| P | Same full objective, fixed-order decoder and search budget as B3. For the same proposal list replace the flat preview rate with time- and station-dependent marginal purchase price net of residual surplus. |

Use paired starts and independent random streams for each case and seed. Construction consumes the same recorded budget for B1/B2/B3/P, including when built once and reused. The search uses 12 iteration and 5 decoded-candidate caps per iteration in addition to the wall limits. Seeds 11, 29, 47, 71 and 101 are development. Seeds 131 and 173 are the separate search-seed validation panel. The budget is 10 s for 20/40, 30 s for 60 and 60 s for 80. A shared deterministic budget cap applies equally to B3/P, including preview scoring, decoding and final independent replay. Measure total wall time and record a deadline exit separately from infeasibility. B0 has no shared start because it is the historical start algorithm.

Pre-comparison runtime amendment. All 14 initial 80-request starts hit 10 s with unprocessed requests. A single 20 s construction rerun per affected seed was frozen in `configs/experiment_stage2_construction_rescue.yaml` before the diagnostic. Both attempts are preserved and both elapsed times are deducted from the original 60 s budget. No market, routing, charge limit or method parameter changed.

First run three diagnostic batches from the same complete incumbent per seed. Evaluate the union of the two ranked shortlists with the same full monetary decoder, and compare against charge-only optimization of the same passenger order. Log overlap, changed choices, duration, all tried moves, exact travel/user/energy deltas and gain counts. The development panel then records all retained failed and successful cases. Keep per-run schedules with model and source hash, construction evidence, violation checks and move traces. Replay saved schedules independently before verdict.

Primary comparison computes (B3 − P) / B3 × 100 for common-feasible merges sharing instance file, seed and start. Report all denominators and failures. Aggregate within each instance then over distinct configurations as well as over run rows. Report range and median across configurations and sensitivity to dropping each configuration. No significance claim from repeated seeds. Include counts of price-induced shortlist changes and accepted deltas. Report B0/B1/B2/B3/P separately. Keep monetary raw components, minutes, energy, SOC and feasibility separately.

**GO threshold.** Median common-feasible paired objective improvement at least 0.5% against B3, positive direction in at least half of distinct common-feasible configurations, no systematic feasibility loss, real price-induced choices, reliable replay, at least four unchanged feasible size/tariff configurations spanning multiple sizes, and credible larger-cohort feasibility. Weak or mixed mechanism evidence, or unresolved original-input interpretation and scaling, can support only CONDITIONAL. Absence of meaningful improvement supports NO-GO for the superiority claim. No adjustment of tariffs, battery, fleet, or visit counts to pursue the threshold.
