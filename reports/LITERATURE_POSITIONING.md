# Literature positioning

The Porto v4 release provides original LEC profiles, fleet parameters and a reproducible routing benchmark. Almeida et al. §3, “Problem definition and LEC extension” and “Instance generation and scaling”, give the cost components and instance construction. The article supplies structural validation and source material. The November 23 search by exact DOI, phrase and author combinations identified no verified public solver outcomes for the exact Porto v4 LEC files. The search scope limits this finding. [Data article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13396958/) · [Official release](https://zenodo.org/records/20707207)

| Related work | Established overlap and distinction |
|---|---|
| Bresich, Raidl and Limmer (GECCO 2024), §§3–5 | On-the-fly charging insertion, fragment-based timing and LNS with classical travel and user excess. Their §3.1 explicitly permits only empty vehicles at stations and applies a fleet-wide visit limit under the stated formulation. Reusing the ideas of paired search or charging insertion does not distinguish this project. [Author PDF](https://www.honda-ri.de/pubs/pdf/5818.pdf) |
| Bresich, Raidl and Limmer (ACM TELO 2026), §§3–6, especially §§4–5 | The revisit extends OTF decoding and considers costly-request destroy operators, exact/heuristic insertion, cached fragments, and larger benchmarks. Merely targeting expensive existing routes or adding lookahead is already closely related. The tested question here is incremental ranking using time-, site- and residual-surplus-dependent price in a matched decoder. [Journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3779426) · [Author PDF](https://www.honda-ri.de/pubs/pdf/6507.pdf) |
| Dong, Luo, Huang, Hu and Qin (TR-E 2025), abstract, formulation and algorithm | Joint dial-a-ride routing, partial charging, ride time, variable ToU prices, and adaptive LNS with dynamic-programming charging already exist. A possible distinction is finite station-period community surplus and evidence from a mechanism-matched ranking comparison on Porto v4. This work rules out a broad claim of introducing pricing into e-DARP. [TR-E article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tre.2024.103946) |
| Almeida et al., Integrating Autonomous Electric Vehicle Charging Stations in Local Energy Communities | Their community-market formulation establishes overlap in station participation and interaction with other prosumers. This pilot uses the released station profiles as fixed inputs to passenger routing rather than optimizing a community market. [Author PDF](https://www.honda-ri.de/pubs/pdf/6276.pdf) |
| Faia et al. (Electronics 2021), §§2–3 | Local-market transactions between prosumers and EVs and coordinated charging have precedent. The paper concerns purchase and scheduling in the energy market, with different operational inputs from paired pickup/dropoff moves. [Paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/10/2/129) |

The hypothesis is a measurable improvement from price-dependent paired-move selection under the same feasible charging options and monetary acceptance as B3. It needs an experimental ablation and corroboration on a credible cohort before a scientific contribution can be claimed.
