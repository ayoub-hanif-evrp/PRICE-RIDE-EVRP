# Casablanca transfer protocol

Casablanca is a future simulation target. Porto remains the independent benchmark. Use the frozen directed Casablanca city relation 4072985, GraphML SHA-256 `60af359a9dcec1371b4984a97f6d2c5536d6542ae7f420f6c778523d299fa73f`, and the original Rouky–Bousouf workbook with its published TTI. The graph has 38,115 nodes and 100,734 edges with 37,918 nodes in the largest strongly connected component. The workbook covers 440 directed sampled OD pairs across 22 communes. Both endpoints of 352 pairs lie inside the city polygon. These inputs provide sample traffic indices and a routing network. ## Synthetic scenarios and split

- Generate hypothetical pickup anchors with equal commune weight as a neutral design assumption for covered communes. Restrict candidates to the largest SCC inside the city. Until polygon-level commune joins and sample coverage are audited, identify groups by nearest validated in-boundary workbook anchor, never by cartographic assertion. Reject or record distances above a predeclared 300 m snap limit. Sampling graph nodes directly avoids snapping a generated node to a disconnected road. Audit the eligible counts per anchor and the sampling weights. Choose dropoffs from eligible nodes along a directed path of 0.5–8 km. Depos and candidate station sites are scenario nodes with inspected accessibility, with no assertion of existing fleet or EV infrastructure.
- Use static directed shortest-path lengths in metres. Start with 30 km/h and a fixed 0.18 kWh/km traction assumption. At that speed the static evaluator's proportional coefficient is 0.09 kWh/min. If a congestion stress variant slows travel but retains distance energy, extend the evaluator to accept an explicit energy matrix before running it. A static travel-time matrix cannot represent varying departure-dependent travel times. Any future such model needs FIFO and cross-period tests.
- Generate hypothetical departure anchors within daytime sampling bands 07:00–10:00 and 16:00–20:00, equal counts by band. Use 30 min pickup windows, delivery windows offset by the shortest direct travel plus service slack, 3 min service, and a 60 min ride limit. Build and replay small development scenarios with 4, 8 and 12 requests, two 4-seat vehicles, 42 kWh capacity and 50% reserve. Example starting energy 30 kWh and candidate power 18 kW are proposed scenario assumptions, to be frozen before any future comparison. Follow the same controls and feasible-replay procedure. Deployment and fleet assumptions require separate sensitivity.
- Use 301/307/311 for future scenario development and 401/409/419 for a separate locked scenario split. The current fixture seed 20261123 belongs to neither set. Do not select locked scenarios based on cost outcomes.
- Use TTI only for descriptive coverage initially. A later static stress variant can assign a day/hour/commune median by nearest verified anchor, with no interpolation beyond supported commune/hour combinations and a labeled full-coverage fallback scenario if needed. Keep the unaggregated coverage, dispersion and snap tails. TTI is a trajectory-level proxy, and the workbook does not establish a link to each OSM routing edge. Exclude the mixed-scale raw travel-time columns.

## Electricity and validation

No Casablanca charger tariff or solar supplies are calibrated here. ONEE's national medium-voltage table is a reference for identifying contract variables. Before applying any rate, confirm the local distributor, contract and voltage class, date, taxes, capacity fees, timezone and seasonal period rule. If this cannot be verified, use explicitly hypothetical low/medium/high MAD/kWh profiles with parameters fixed before the study. Track MAD and operational quantities separately, including a unit-neutral cost adapter before using the monetary Porto reporting code. PVGIS could support scenario production under a declared PV size, loss and local-load model. Begin with a no-solar scenario. PVGIS produces modeled potential and gives no observed site surplus. [ONEE tariff reference](https://www.one.org.ma/FR/pages/interne.asp?esp=1&id1=14&id2=114&t2=1) · [EU JRC PVGIS](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/photovoltaic-geographical-information-system-pvgis_en)

The fixture validates checksums, directed graph/SCC inventory, published TTI coverage, distances from 12 in-boundary sampled endpoints to the nearest SCC node, and synthetic graph-native service via independent replay. The generated windows in this fixture follow a known sequence solely to validate the adapter. Objective rates are disabled in the fixture. Its stage-specific assumptions include one vehicle, two hypothetical requests, zero surplus and 42 kWh capacity with 30 kWh initially.
